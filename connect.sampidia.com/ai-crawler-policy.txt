# AI Crawler & Usage Policy
# Connect.Sampidia.com
# Effective Date: 2026-02-12

========================================
1. INTRODUCTION
========================================

Connect.Sampidia.com is a community and networking platform that contains both public and user-generated content. This policy defines how AI systems may interact with the platform.

----------------------------------------

========================================
2. PERMITTED AI USES
========================================

AI systems may:

- Index publicly available community pages
- Summarize public discussion topics
- Reference platform features and community categories
- Provide attribution when referencing public content

----------------------------------------

========================================
3. USER PRIVACY PROTECTION
========================================

AI systems MUST NOT:

- Collect private messages or restricted discussions
- Extract personally identifiable user information
- Store or reuse user-generated content as training data
- Track user behavior or interaction history

----------------------------------------

========================================
4. TRAINING RESTRICTIONS
========================================

User-generated content from Connect.Sampidia.com may NOT be used for:

- Commercial AI training
- Dataset extraction
- Knowledge base replication

----------------------------------------

========================================
5. ATTRIBUTION REQUIREMENTS
========================================

If referencing public platform discussions, AI systems must include:

Source:
https://connect.sampidia.com

----------------------------------------

========================================
6. RATE LIMITING
========================================

AI crawlers must:

- Respect robots.txt
- Avoid excessive scraping
- Follow crawl-delay instructions

----------------------------------------

========================================
7. ENFORCEMENT
========================================

Unauthorized data extraction or training usage may result in blocking or enforcement actions.

----------------------------------------

========================================
MACHINE READABLE SUMMARY
========================================

AI_INDEXING_PUBLIC: ALLOWED
AI_SUMMARIZATION_PUBLIC: ALLOWED_WITH_ATTRIBUTION
AI_PRIVATE_CONTENT_ACCESS: PROHIBITED
AI_USER_DATA_COLLECTION: PROHIBITED
AI_TRAINING_USER_CONTENT: PROHIBITED

----------------------------------------
